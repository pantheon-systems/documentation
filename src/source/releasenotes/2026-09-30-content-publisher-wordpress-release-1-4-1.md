Version 1.4.1 of the [Pantheon Content Publisher](https://wordpress.org/plugins/pantheon-content-publisher/) WordPress plugin is now available.

## What's new?

Improved how Smart Components are placed in your posts and pages, including in the Content Publisher preview. [#261](https://github.com/pantheon-systems/pantheon-content-publisher-wordpress/pull/261)

Update to 1.4.1 from the WordPress dashboard under **Plugins > Installed Plugins**, or download it from the [WordPress Plugin Repository](https://wordpress.org/plugins/pantheon-content-publisher/).

For more details, see the [plugin changelog](https://github.com/pantheon-systems/pantheon-content-publisher-wordpress/releases).
