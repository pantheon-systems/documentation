## What is the vulnerability?

The PHP project released [8.2.31](https://www.php.net/ChangeLog-8.php#8.2.31), [8.3.31](https://www.php.net/ChangeLog-8.php#8.3.31), [8.4.21](https://www.php.net/ChangeLog-8.php#8.4.21), and [8.5.6](https://www.php.net/ChangeLog-8.php#8.5.6) with security fixes, along with bug fixes and enhancements. See the PHP changelogs for the individual fixes.

## Are sites on Pantheon affected?

Sites running an earlier patch release were affected until the platform update reached them.

## What Pantheon has done

Pantheon updated PHP 8.2, 8.3, 8.4, and 8.5 on the platform to the patched releases. Updates are applied automatically over a few days.

## What you need to do

No action is needed.
