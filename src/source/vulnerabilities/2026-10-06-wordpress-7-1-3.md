## What is the vulnerability?

[WordPress 7.1.3](https://wordpress.org/news/2026/10/wordpress-7-1-3-maintenance-and-security-release/) resolves seven security vulnerabilities:

- A stored XSS on the Comments administration page, exploitable through pending comments.
- A denial of service issue in the `WP_Http::make_absolute_url()` method.
- A second-order SQL injection in WordPress WXR export.
- A weakness that allows users with the Author role to sticky posts.
- Unauthenticated disclosure of comments on private and unpublished posts.
- XSS in Imgur embeds.
- Forgeable parameters passed to the `{status}_{type}` hook, which can lead to action name collision.

## Are sites on Pantheon affected?

Yes. WordPress sites running a version before 7.1.3 are affected until you update.

## What Pantheon has done

Pantheon deployed a platform-wide mitigation (virtual patching through the routing network) against external abuse of the stored XSS on the Comments administration page, and monitors that rule. The mitigation doesn't replace the update.

## What you need to do

Update to WordPress 7.1.3 as soon as possible from your Pantheon dashboard or Terminus. See [how to apply core updates](/core-updates#apply-upstream-updates-via-the-site-dashboard).
