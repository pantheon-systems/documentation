# City opens new library branch

The city opened its fourth library branch on Saturday, adding 12,000 square feet of reading space on the east side. Mayor Dana Ortiz cut the ribbon, according to a city press release.

The branch holds 40,000 books and offers free internet access. The library board said it will open from 9 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays. Funding came from a 2024 bond measure approved by voters.

Residents can apply for a library card at the front desk with a photo ID.
