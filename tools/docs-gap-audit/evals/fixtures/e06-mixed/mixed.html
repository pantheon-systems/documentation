Widget overview

Follow these steps to start:

  1. Open the dashboard.
  2. Choose a widget.
  3. Select Share.

Why widgets are stored per account

Widgets are stored per account because teams share them. The trade-off is that deleting one affects everyone, which is why the design keeps a recovery window of 30 days.
